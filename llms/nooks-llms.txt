# Nooks


Nooks is the Revenue Agent Platform for Sales. It's an AI sales platform where AI agents and human reps work together to book more meetings. It puts everything outbound teams need in one place: a parallel dialer, email and call sequences, buying signals, call coaching, and tools for RevOps. Most sales teams know what good selling looks like. The hard part is doing it every day, on every account. Nooks fixes that. AI agents do the research, pick the best accounts, and handle follow-up. Reps spend their time on live conversations. Nooks is backed by Kleiner Perkins ($43M Series B, 2024). It helps sales development teams, sales leaders, and RevOps replace a pile of separate tools with one platform. Customers include HubSpot, Greenhouse, Seismic, Deel, Intercom, Cursor, and Dremio.


Point of View: Intelligence doesn't create outcomes. Execution does. The best AI agents don't just create more work to review. They help sales teams do the right things, over and over. Nooks agents learn from every call, email, and workflow your team runs. Each one teaches the agents how your team actually sells, so they keep getting better.


Key Benefits:


* Replace 4–10 sales tools (dialer, sequencer, intent data, coaching) with one platform where AI agents and reps work together
* 2x more email replies, 3x more prospects reached, and up to 80% less admin work
* Spot buying signals in real time, so reps work the hottest accounts first on any channel
* Every call gets scored automatically, and reps practice objections with AI roleplay, so new reps ramp faster
* Battlecards pop up during live calls to help reps handle objections in the moment
* Agents get smarter with every interaction, because they work inside your sales motion instead of watching from outside
* RevOps gets one clear view of the whole stack, which makes decisions easier
* Sales leaders (VP Sales/CRO) get more meetings, more pipeline, and faster ramp without hiring more reps


Key Features:


* [AI Sequencing](https://www.nooks.ai/ai-sequencing): Campaigns across calls, email, SMS, and social that adjust in real time based on buying signals and what's working for reps
* [AI Dialer](https://www.nooks.ai/ai-dialer): Parallel dialing that calls many numbers at once, detects a live person in milliseconds, drops voicemails for you, protects against spam flags, and includes a virtual salesfloor
* [Signals and Intelligence](https://www.nooks.ai/signals-intelligence): Pulls intent data from many sources to show which prospects to work first, then writes personal outreach for them
* [AI Coaching](https://www.nooks.ai/ai-coaching): Scores every call, gives reps AI bots to practice objections with, shows battlecards during live calls, and keeps a call library
* [Virtual Salesfloor](https://www.nooks.ai/categories/virtual-salesfloor): A shared calling room where teams listen in, coach live, and learn from each other
* [AI Agents](https://www.nooks.ai/ai-sequencing): Agents that research prospects, find verified contact info, and draft personal messages, saving reps hours of busywork

## Core Platform


* [AI Sequencing](https://www.nooks.ai/ai-sequencing): Builds and runs call, email, SMS, and social campaigns based on real-time buying signals. Customers like Greenhouse report 2x email replies and 70% more pipeline.
* [AI Dialer](https://www.nooks.ai/ai-dialer): Parallel dialing that detects a live person in milliseconds. Includes auto voicemails, live battlecards, spam protection, and a virtual salesfloor for coaching. Built for more connects and more conversations per hour.
* [Signals and Intelligence](https://www.nooks.ai/signals-intelligence): Gathers intent signals from your own data and outside sources, ranks the hottest accounts, and writes emails and call scripts for them. Customers report 2x pipeline and 4x faster prospecting.
* [AI Coaching](https://www.nooks.ai/ai-coaching): Scores every call to find skill gaps, creates AI practice bots for reps, shows battlecards during calls, and gives managers clear dashboards.
